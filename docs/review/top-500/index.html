Every geographic event sequence in this cohort was inspected in descending order of its original atlas count. Suspected errors were checked against selected source biographies. Corrections include homonymous towns, invalid jurisdictions, appointments never taken up and biographies containing several people.

This is a completed mapping inspection, not a certification of 500 biographies. Source adjudication remains open where the evidence does not yet support a correction. The working notes retain those questions. Confirmed composite records are excluded from office and movement assertions while their component careers await reconstruction.

The map follows appointments, including acting and temporary service. Explicit conference participation and visits remain in the written record. Appointment scope audit. A count is a distinct directed corridor inferred from dated appointments. It is not a count of independently verified journeys. The revised atlas retains named localities. Solid lines follow dated sequences; dashed lines show alternative connections where within-year order or intervening locations remain unresolved. Possible connections are counted separately and do not assert additional journeys. Source-reviewed dates and explicit sequences restore within-year connections. A legal change at the same coordinate is not a move.
