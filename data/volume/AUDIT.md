# Volume roster extraction — cross-edition audit

Era notes (verified against the printed volumes, July 2026): 1946 and
1952 print NO Record of Services — 1946 was the restricted first
post-war edition and 1952 says so explicitly ("Staff lists have been
restricted generally, and the Record of Services section has had to
[be omitted]"; the section is a note only) — so bios=0 there is the
volume, not the parser. From 1952 on, colony chapters carry only a
compact senior CIVIL ESTABLISHMENT in run-together "Post—Name"
paragraphs (western name order, no salaries): post-war record counts
are genuinely an order of magnitude below interwar establishments.
